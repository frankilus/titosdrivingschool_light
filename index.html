How it works

Three steps to your license

No guessing, no package you don't need. Tell us where you're starting from and we'll map the shortest route to test day.

The DMV requires the 5-hour pre-licensing class before you can schedule a road test. Ours runs online — we send you a link and you join from home.

Lessons on real Queens streets — parking, turns, test routes — until you're ready. Students regularly pass on the first try.
